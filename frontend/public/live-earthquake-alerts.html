🧮 Seismic Logic & Formula

Primary Radius

R₁ (km) = max(2, 10 + 8×(M - 5) - 0.15×DepthKm)

Secondary Radius

R₂ (km) = R₁ × 1.8

Felt Radius

Rfelt (km) = R₁ × 3.2

Affected Area

Area (km²) = π × (R₂)²
